# Code of conduct

Rollout Diff welcomes participation regardless of background, identity, or
experience. Treat people with respect and discuss technical disagreements
constructively.

## Standards

- Give useful feedback and respect differing perspectives.
- Respect privacy, consent, and professional boundaries.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and disclosure of
  private information without permission are unacceptable.

These standards apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces, including maintainers' conduct and public representation of
the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to Mamadou Wane at
[mamadouswane@gmail.com](mailto:mamadouswane@gmail.com). Include relevant links
and context. Reports will be reviewed discreetly, with information shared only
as needed to assess and address the concern.

Maintainers may request corrections, remove content, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation according to the severity and recurrence of
the behavior. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
